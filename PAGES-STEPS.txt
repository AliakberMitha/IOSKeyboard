PUT THE LSD WEB APP ONLINE WITH GITHUB PAGES (free)
===================================================
1. github.com > + > New repository. Name it: lsd  (public). Create.
2. "uploading an existing file" > drag EVERYTHING from this zip
   (index.html, manifest.json, assets folder, stickers folder,
    PAGES-STEPS.txt is optional) > Commit changes.
3. Repo > Settings > Pages (left menu).
   Under "Build and deployment": Source = Deploy from a branch,
   Branch = main, folder = / (root). Save.
4. Wait 1-2 minutes, refresh the Pages screen - it shows your link:
   https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/lsd/
5. Share that link in your WhatsApp groups.

FOR IPHONE USERS (tell them this):
- Open the link in Safari.
- Tap Share (square with arrow) > "Add to Home Screen".
- An LSD icon appears like an app. Open it, type, tap Share Image
  or a sticker, choose WhatsApp, pick the chat, send.

Works on Android and computers too - same link.
